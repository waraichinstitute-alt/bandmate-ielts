Questions, answered

Is this an official IELTS product?

No. SpeakReady is an independent preparation service — not affiliated with or endorsed by IELTS partners. We teach the public test format; only a qualified examiner can award an official band score.

How does the practice estimate work?

Your recording is analysed on your own device: speaking time, pace, pauses, filler words and answer length. That produces a practice range (like 6.0–6.5), never a single exact score — and never an official result.

Will you judge my accent?

Never. We only care whether your speech is easy to understand. Pronunciation feedback is conservative and accent is never penalised.

Where does my recording go?

Solo practice recordings never leave your device — analysis happens in your browser. Partner calls are direct, encrypted phone-to-phone video. You can delete any recording or all your data at any time.

Does it cover Academic and General Training?

Yes — the Speaking test format is identical for both.

Can I cancel?

There is nothing to cancel. The core product is free with no card and no subscription.

Privacy

Your voice stays yours

  • Microphone: we ask for it only to record your practice answers. We use your microphone only to record this practice answer. You can delete it anytime.
  • Solo practice: recordings and transcripts never leave your device. Analysis runs entirely in your browser.
  • Partner calls: video and audio travel directly between the two phones over an encrypted connection. Our server only introduces you — it never sees or hears the call.
  • Storage: your profile, progress and practice history live in this browser's local storage. There is no account and no cloud copy.
  • Deletion: delete any recording from its results page, or wipe everything via Progress → Delete all my data.
  • Analytics: we don't run third-party analytics. What you do here stays here.

Terms of use

1. What this is. SpeakReady is an independent IELTS Speaking practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the IELTS partners, and nothing here is an official test or official score.

2. Practice estimates. Any band range shown is a practice estimate based on on-device measurements of your recording. It is not an official IELTS result, and only a qualified IELTS examiner can award an official band score.

3. Age. You must be 18 or older to use partner video calls.

4. Be kind. Partner calls are with real learners. Harassment, hate speech, nudity or spam will get you blocked. Use Report any time — it ends the call immediately.

5. Your data. Solo recordings stay on your device; you can delete them any time. See the Privacy page for details.

6. No guarantees. We can't promise a particular band score — only useful practice. The service is provided as-is.
